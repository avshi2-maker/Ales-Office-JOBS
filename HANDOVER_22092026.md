## 1. What this app is
Mobile-first field tool for **Ales** (the fabricator) to document projects on site.
Ales opens it on his phone → picks a job type → fills fields with the customer →
shoots photos/video from the phone camera → saves. Everything lands in **one gallery**
that Avshi/Ales filter and share to potential customers via WhatsApp or mail.

Four job lines: **כיורים** (sinks) · **שיפוצים** (renovation, פנים/חוץ) ·
**דלתות שיש** (marble doors) · **המלצות** (testimonials).

Language: Hebrew RTL UI. Private (PIN gate + `noindex`).
